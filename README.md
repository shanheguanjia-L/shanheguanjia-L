## Projects

### [fit-plan](https://github.com/shanheguanjia-L/fit-plan) · [Try it](https://shanheguanjia-l.github.io/fit-plan/)

A chat-style fitness helper: answer a few questions and get a weekly training plan and a 7-day meal example.
Everything is calculated in the browser — nothing is uploaded. The page is in Simplified Chinese.

健身小助手：回答幾個問題，在手機瀏覽器裡算出每週訓練安排和 7 天飲食示例，資料不上傳。

- Exercise notes and images adapted from [wger](https://wger.de); food data from USDA FoodData Central
- License: code MIT, exercise content and images CC BY-SA 4.0 ([details](https://github.com/shanheguanjia-L/fit-plan/blob/main/LICENSE))

### [context-islands](https://github.com/shanheguanjia-L/context-islands) · [Try it (English)](https://shanheguanjia-l.github.io/context-islands/en/)

A 12-week, game-style course on prompt engineering and context engineering for complete beginners.
Each "island" hides one way AI fails; you beat it by predicting, experimenting and explaining *why* it happens — Octalysis × Feynman technique × Pomodoro.
Progress stays in your browser. Available in English, Traditional Chinese and Simplified Chinese.

脈絡群島：給零基礎職場人士的 12 週提示詞工程與脈絡工程互動課程。每座島藏著一種 AI 失效方式，要先預測、做實驗、用 12 歲孩子聽得懂的話講出原理才能通關。

- License: CC BY-SA 4.0 ([details](https://github.com/shanheguanjia-L/context-islands/blob/main/LICENSE))
